Since 2011

The Journey of Light & Vision

LUMINA was born from a simple yet powerful belief: that sunglasses should be more than just an accessory. They are an extension of your personality, a shield for your eyes, and a testament to your taste.

Founded in New York City by a team of optical engineers and fashion designers, we set out to create eyewear that marries precision optics with high fashion. Every frame is a result of hundreds of hours of design, prototyping, and meticulous hand-finishing.

"We do not just make sunglasses. We craft instruments of vision that celebrate the art of seeing."

From sourcing the finest Italian acetates to partnering with Japanese lens manufacturers, every component of a LUMINA frame is chosen with intention. Our commitment to quality extends beyond the product — it is reflected in every interaction, every detail, and every moment of light.

Our Mission

To illuminate the world through exceptional eyewear that combines cutting-edge optical technology with uncompromising design. We strive to protect and enhance your vision while elevating your personal style.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in luxury eyewear globally — synonymous with quality, innovation, and timeless elegance. We envision a world where everyone can experience the clarity of premium vision.

Milestones

Our Journey

2011

The Beginning

LUMINA founded in New York City with a vision to redefine luxury eyewear. First collection launched with 12 handcrafted styles.

2014

First Flagship Store

Opened our flagship store on Madison Avenue. Expanded to 50+ styles and introduced the iconic Solaris collection.

2017

Global Expansion

Launched in 15 countries across Europe and Asia. Partnered with premium retailers and received international acclaim.

2021

Innovation Milestone

Introduced patented FlexFit technology and eco-friendly acetate line. Surpassed 30,000 happy customers worldwide.

2026

New Horizon

Launched the Sport Elite collection with carbon fiber frames. Achieved 4.9-star rating across all platforms. Over 500 styles available.
